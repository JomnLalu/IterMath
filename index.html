Short drills for every calculation from kindergarten to grade 12, from number bonds to integrals. Answer, get instant feedback, and see the full working whenever you miss.

Number sense

  • +? Number bonds Find the missing part that makes 5, 10 or 20. Grades K–1
  • 1·0 Place value Find what a digit is worth and build numbers from expanded form. Grades 1–4
  • ≈ Rounding Round whole numbers to the nearest 10, 100 or 1,000, and decimals to a set place. Grades 3–5

Arithmetic

  • + Addition Add two 2-digit numbers. Grades 1–2
  • − Subtraction Subtract one 2-digit number from another. Answers are never negative. Grades 1–2
  • × Multiplication Times tables from 2 × 2 to 12 × 12. Grades 3–4
  • ÷ Division Divide by 2 to 12. Every answer is a whole number. Grades 3–4
  • R Division with remainders Divide and give the quotient and remainder, like 7 R 3. Grades 3–5
  • 12× Long multiplication Multiply 2- and 3-digit numbers by splitting into tens and ones. Grades 4–5
  • 12÷ Long division Divide 3- and 4-digit numbers by 1- and 2-digit divisors. Every answer is whole. Grades 4–6
  • ( ) Mixed operations Expressions that combine all four operations. Answers can be negative. Grades 5–7

Integers, powers and factors

  • ± Integers Add, subtract, multiply and divide negative numbers, and work with absolute value. Grades 6–7
  • an Exponents Evaluate powers, including negative bases, zero and negative exponents. Grades 5–8
  • √ Square and cube roots Find square roots of perfect squares and cube roots of perfect cubes. Grades 6–8
  • GCF GCF and LCM Find the greatest common factor and least common multiple of two numbers. Grades 4–6
  • 2·3 Prime factorization Write a number as a product of primes. Grades 5–7

Fractions and decimals

  • ½ Equivalent fractions Simplify fractions and fill in the missing part of an equivalent fraction. Grades 3–5
  • 1½ Mixed numbers Convert between improper fractions and mixed numbers. Grades 4–5
  • ½+ Adding and subtracting fractions Like and unlike denominators, and mixed numbers. Give answers in simplest form. Grades 4–6
  • ½× Multiplying and dividing fractions Multiply and divide fractions and whole numbers. Give answers in simplest form. Grades 5–6
  • .5+ Adding and subtracting decimals Add and subtract decimals to the hundredths. Grades 4–5
  • .5× Multiplying and dividing decimals Multiply and divide decimals by whole numbers and by other decimals. Grades 5–6
  • ¼% Fractions, decimals and percents Convert between fractions, decimals and percents. Grades 6–7

Ratios and percents

  • 2:3 Ratios and proportions Simplify ratios, find equivalent ratios, share amounts and solve proportions. Grades 6–7
  • /h Unit rates and speed Find the price of one, speed, distance and time. Grades 6–7
  • % Percentages Find a percent of a number, what percent one number is of another, and the whole. Grades 6–7
  • ±% Percent change, discount and tax Percent increase and decrease, sale prices, sales tax and tips. Grades 7–8
  • $ Simple and compound interest Simple interest with I = Prt and compound interest, rounded to the cent. Grades 7–12

Geometry and measurement

  • cm Unit conversion Convert metric and customary units of length, mass, capacity and time. Grades 3–6
  • ▭ Area and perimeter Rectangles, squares, triangles, parallelograms and trapezoids. Grades 3–7
  • ∠ Angles Complementary, supplementary, triangle and polygon angles. Grades 4–8
  • m³ Volume and surface area Prisms, cubes, pyramids, cylinders, cones and spheres. Grades 5–8
  • πr² Circles Circumference and area from the radius or diameter. Grades 7–8
  • ⊿ Pythagorean theorem Find a missing side of a right triangle with a² + b² = c². Grades 8–9
  • (x,y) Coordinate geometry Distance, midpoint and slope between two points. Grades 6–10

Statistics and probability

  • avg Mean, median, mode and range Find the center and spread of a small data set. Grades 6–7
  • P(A) Probability Marbles, dice and coins, including two events. Give answers as fractions. Grades 7–12
  • nCr Combinations Count the ways to choose items when order does not matter. Sets of 4 to 9. Grades 10–12
  • nPr Permutations Count the ways to arrange items when order matters. Sets of 3 to 8. Grades 10–12

Algebra

  • 3x Evaluating expressions Substitute values for variables and simplify. Grades 6–7
  • x+1 One-step equations Solve x + a = b, x − a = b, ax = b and x/a = b. Grades 6–7
  • 2x+1 Two-step equations Solve equations like 3x + 5 = 20 and x/4 − 2 = 3. Grades 7–8
  • x=x Multi-step equations Variables on both sides, brackets and like terms. Grades 8–9
  • < Inequalities Solve linear inequalities, flipping the sign when dividing by a negative. Grades 7–9
  • mx+b Linear functions Slope, intercepts and values of y = mx + b and Ax + By = C. Grades 8–9
  • { } Systems of equations Solve two linear equations in x and y by elimination. Grades 8–9
  • xn Exponent rules Product, quotient and power rules, with zero and negative exponents. Grades 8–9
  • 10n Scientific notation Convert to and from a × 10ⁿ, and multiply numbers in scientific notation. Grades 8–9
  • x²+ Polynomials Add, subtract and multiply polynomials. Type answers like 3x^2 − 2x + 5. Grades 9–10
  • ()() Factoring quadratics Factor trinomials, differences of squares and perfect squares. Grades 9–10
  • x² Quadratic equations Solve by factoring, square roots and the quadratic formula. Grades 9–10
  • a√b Simplifying radicals Simplify square roots, multiply them and combine like radicals. Grades 9–10
  • f(x) Functions Evaluate functions, compose them and use inverses. Grades 9–11

Advanced algebra and trigonometry

  • log Logarithms Evaluate logs, use log rules and solve exponential equations. Grades 10–12
  • an Sequences and series Arithmetic and geometric sequences: next term, nth term and sums. Grades 9–12
  • i Complex numbers Add, subtract, multiply and divide complex numbers, and powers of i. Grades 10–12
  • sin Right-triangle trigonometry Use sine, cosine and tangent to find sides and angles. Grades 9–11
  • π Unit circle and radians Exact sine, cosine and tangent values, and converting degrees and radians. Grades 10–12
  • [ ] Matrices 2 × 2 and 3 × 3 determinants and 2 × 2 matrix multiplication. Grades 11–12

Calculus

  • lim Limits Direct substitution, removable discontinuities and limits at infinity. Grades 11–12
  • f′ Derivatives Power rule for polynomials, slopes of tangents and the chain rule. Grades 11–12
  • ∫ Integrals Antiderivatives of polynomials and definite integrals. Grades 11–12
