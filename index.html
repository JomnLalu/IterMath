Short math drills for arithmetic and counting. Answer, get instant feedback, and see the full working whenever you miss.

Arithmetic

  • + Addition Add two 2-digit numbers.
  • − Subtraction Subtract one 2-digit number from another. Answers are never negative.
  • × Multiplication Times tables from 2 × 2 to 12 × 12.
  • ÷ Division Divide by 2 to 12. Every answer is a whole number.
  • ( ) Mixed operations Expressions that combine all four operations. Answers can be negative.

Counting

  • nCr Combinations Count the ways to choose items when order does not matter. Sets of 4 to 9.
  • nPr Permutations Count the ways to arrange items when order matters. Sets of 3 to 8.
